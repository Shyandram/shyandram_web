About

Currently a PhD candidate in Computer Science at NYCU, I hold dual Master's degrees in ECE from UWM and EL from CYCU. My research expertise lies in Image Processing, Pattern Recognition, and Deep Learning, with a focus on Image Restoration and Object Detection. I care about the gap between a promising model and a system that holds up in the real world.

  • 01Restore

    Recover detail, color, and visibility from degraded imagery.

  • 02Recognize

    Turn visual signals into reliable, useful information.

  • 03Deploy

    Keep models efficient enough for practical environments.
